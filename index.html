A Little Love from Paati's Kitchen

Paati Kadai began in 2019 with Kamala Paati's passion for traditional homemade sweets. What started in her own kitchen has grown into a little place where every recipe carries the warmth of family traditions.

We believe that good sweets begin with good ingredients. That's why we use pure ghee, prepare everything fresh after you order, and never use preservatives.

Every box is prepared with care, just like Paati makes it for her family.
